Farm advice in your own language.

Kishan tells you when to sow, how much water to give, what is wrong with your crop, and where to sell it for a better price. Speak or type in your language.

Open KishanFree. Works on any phone browser and installs like an app.

What Kishan does

⛅Weather for farm work

A 10-day forecast for your exact location, with the best days to sow, water and harvest.

📷Crop photo check

Take a photo of a leaf or plant. Get the likely pest, disease or missing nutrient and what to do next.

💧Water planner

Litres needed this week for your crop, soil and irrigation method, after counting the coming rain.

📈Mandi prices

Today’s prices from mandis in your state, the best place to sell, and what your harvest may earn.

🎙️Voice helper

Ask any farming question by speaking. Kishan answers aloud in your language.

🧪Sprays and fertilizer

From a photo, the pesticide, weedicide or fertilizer to use, with the quantity for your acres.
